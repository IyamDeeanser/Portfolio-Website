Now building

Goblet

00  Overview

A regeneratively cooled flight engine

Goblet is one of the first regeneratively cooled flight engines in Canada, including both professionals and amateurs. It has to make 3.5 kN from a 3 inch chamber at an order of magnitude less mass than my previous engines, and survive multiple firings without damage.

3.5 kNthrust
1-2 kgtarget mass
01  Lower Bulkhead

Where the vehicle meets the engine

The bulkhead is an integral part of the thrust assembly and brings both propellant feeds down from the tanks. It caps the bottom of the tanks and holds both fuel and oxidizer tubes.

02  MOV and MFV

Main valves

Servo driven ball valves control the main oxidizer and main fuel flow, mounted directly above the engine.

03  Chamber PT

Pressure transducer

A pressure tapoff through the injector means that chamber pressure can be measured directly through this pressure transducer.

04  Igniter

Solid Igniter

A drilled-out C6 motor fires through the center of the injector to light the mixing propellant flows.

05  Injector

6x Like Doublets

The injector was the most complex and time consuming component to design. Over 80 simulations and a year of iteration resulted in this critical-flow, like-doublet injector.

80+CFD iterations
2 PSImanifold differential
06  Chamber

Regeneratively Cooled

The chamber is printed in AlSi10Mg to fit a 5″ fincan. Fuel runs down the integrated inlet and back up 54 cooling channels inside the walls before reaching the injector, with a steady state of under 300C.

~300Cliner temperature
